# Field node bill of materials

- ESP32-class board
- Capacitive soil-moisture probe
- BME280 or equivalent temperature/relative-humidity sensor
- Optional waterproof soil-temperature probe
- Optional tipping-bucket rain gauge
- Optional water-level sensor
- Solar panel, charge controller, protected Li-ion battery, and fuse
- Weather-resistant enclosure, cable glands, mounting stake, and strain relief

Availability, prices, and environmental ratings require local procurement review.
